      Dust along the route

      How to read the labels . OBSERVED: retrieved from an orbiter instrument for that cell and those sols. HISTORICAL: statistics of observations from other Martian Years. INTERPOLATED: estimated between observations (kriging), shown only when asked for. MODELLED: no dust value here is a model output. This is detection from past observations and a historical record, not a forecast. Optical depth describes the whole air column from orbit; it does not measure visibility or conditions on the ground.
