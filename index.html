          Dust monitor

            Dust along the route

            How to read the labels . OBSERVED: retrieved from an orbiter instrument for that cell and those sols. HISTORICAL: statistics of observations from other Martian Years. INTERPOLATED: estimated between observations (kriging), shown only when asked for. MODELLED: no dust value here is a model output. This is detection from past observations and a historical record, not a forecast. Optical depth describes the whole air column from orbit; it does not measure visibility or conditions on the ground.

                How the readouts look

                Units
                Layout

                US units: feet, °F, psi and millirem. The column sits beside the map on wide screens. "Numbers first" swaps the two: the readouts fill the screen in large type and the map becomes a mini-map in the top right corner. On narrow screens the readouts always sit under the map.

                App colours

                The night themes are easier on the eyes in the dark; Night red keeps them adjusted to it. High contrast is for bright light.

                Walker

                Every recorded trail carries its walker's name and kind, and landmark visits are counted per walker.
